Principles

Built for the ummah,
not for engagement.

01

Every word is sourced.

Duas trace to Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi. Translations come from published, licensed translators. Anything without a source doesn't get published — full stop.

02

No tracking. Ever.

No third-party trackers. No fingerprinting. Bookmarks, streaks, and reading progress live only on your device. If we can't verify a privacy claim, we don't make it.

03

Works with no signal.

The Quran, duas, prayer times, and tutorials are cached after your first visit. On the train, on ḥajj, in a village with 2G — everything still opens instantly.
