Learn Ṣalāh · One rakʿah

How to pray, step by step.

A single rakʿah from beginning to end, with the Arabic, transliteration, English, and the classical source for each dhikr. Applies to all five daily prayers.

Step 1 · Niyyah

Intention

Silently, in the heart. No verbal utterance is required.

Standing facing the qiblah, form the intention in the heart to pray the specific prayer (e.g. two rakʿāt of Fajr).

Source: Bukhārī 1 · "Actions are only by intentions."

Step 2 · Takbīrat al-Iḥrām

Opening takbīr

Raise both hands to the level of the ears or shoulders, and say:

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

Allāhu akbar

Allāh is the greatest.

Source: Bukhārī 735 · Muslim 390

Step 3 · Qiyām · Openings

Opening supplication and recitation

Fold the right hand over the left on the chest, then recite:

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ

Subḥānaka Allāhumma wa bi-ḥamdika, wa tabāraka-smuka, wa taʿālā jadduka, wa lā ilāha ghayruk

Glory be to You, O Allāh, and praise. Blessed is Your name and exalted is Your majesty. There is no god but You.

Then say Aʿūdhu billāhi min ash-shayṭāni-r-rajīm, then Bismillāhi-r-raḥmāni-r-raḥīm, then recite Sūrat al-Fātiḥah, then another sūrah (any sūrah of your choosing — commonly the short mufaṣṣal sūrahs).

Sources: Abū Dāwūd 775 (opening duʿāʾ) · Bukhārī 756 (Fātiḥah is a pillar)

Step 4 · Rukūʿ

Bowing

Say Allāhu akbar, then bow with a straight back, hands on the knees, and say (three or more times):

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ

Subḥāna rabbiya-l-ʿaẓīm

Glory be to my Lord, the Magnificent.

Source: Muslim 772 · Abū Dāwūd 869

Step 5 · Iʿtidāl

Standing back up

Rise from rukūʿ saying:

سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ

Samiʿa-llāhu li-man ḥamidah

Allāh hears the one who praises Him.

Once standing, add:

رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ

Rabbanā wa laka-l-ḥamd

Our Lord, and to You is the praise.

Source: Bukhārī 795 · Muslim 411

Step 6 · Sujūd

Prostration

Say Allāhu akbar, then prostrate placing seven parts on the ground (forehead+nose, palms, knees, toes) and say (three or more times):

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَىٰ

Subḥāna rabbiya-l-aʿlā

Glory be to my Lord, the Most High.

Source: Muslim 772 · An-Nasāʾī 1051

Step 7 · Jalsah

Sitting between prostrations

Sit up, saying Allāhu akbar, and say (once or more):

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي

Rabbi-ghfir lī

My Lord, forgive me.

Then Allāhu akbar and prostrate again as in step 6.

Source: Abū Dāwūd 850 · Ibn Mājah 897

Step 8 · Tashahhud + Taslīm

Testimony and closing

After the final rakʿah, sit and recite the tashahhud:

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَىٰ عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

at-Taḥiyyātu lillāh wa-ṣ-ṣalawātu wa-ṭ-ṭayyibāt … ashhadu an lā ilāha illa-llāh, wa ashhadu anna Muḥammadan ʿabduhu wa rasūluh.

All greetings, prayers, and pure things are for Allāh… I bear witness that there is no god but Allāh, and I bear witness that Muḥammad is His servant and messenger.

Follow with the Ibrāhīmī ṣalawāt on the Prophet ﷺ, then turn the head right saying as-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāh, and left with the same. The prayer is complete.

Source: Bukhārī 831 · Muslim 402

Ṣalāh follows a fixed structure. This tutorial documents one rakʿah — multi-rakʿah prayers repeat steps 3–7 with the tashahhud after every 2nd rakʿah. Differences of madhhab (hand placement, ṣadl vs qabḍ, precise wording of duʿāʾ) are all rooted in valid narrations.
